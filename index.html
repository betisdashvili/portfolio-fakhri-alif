About Me

I am a student at SMAN 1 Mojokerto with a strong interest in esports, particularly Mobile Legends: Bang Bang (MLBB). My primary ambition is to develop my skills as a competitive player and work toward reaching the highest level of competition in the game.

My main focus is on studying professional players' gameplay, understanding their decision-making processes, and learning from experienced players who can provide valuable insights into the game. Through continuous learning and practice, I aim to improve my understanding of competitive play and develop a more effective approach to each match.

I primarily play the Mid Lane role, where I am currently working on improving my decision-making skills. I also aim to develop a stronger understanding of macro gameplay, including map awareness, team communication, and strategic coordination. These skills are essential for making informed decisions and contributing effectively to the team's overall performance.

Beyond individual mechanics, I aspire to become a team captain who can also fulfill the responsibilities of a Mid Laner. By combining my gameplay experience with better map awareness and strategic thinking, I aim to anticipate opponents' movements, evaluate different tactical options, and help my team make coordinated decisions throughout a match.

My long-term goal is to pursue a professional esports career and compete at a higher level. I am open to learning from professional players, exchanging strategic insights, and collaborating with other competitive players to improve my abilities. Ultimately, I want to build a sustainable path in esports by continuously developing my skills, contributing to team success, and preparing myself for future professional opportunities.
